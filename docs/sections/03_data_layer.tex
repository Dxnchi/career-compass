\section{Data Persistence Tier: SQLite Architecture}
\subsection{Overview}
The mobile client leverages an embedded SQLite instance managed through a modular Data Access Object (DAO) / CRUD service layer. This facilitates offline availability, instantaneous state retrieval, and asynchronous synchronization with the central matching engine.

\subsection{Relational Entity Schema}
The local relational structure consists of four core tables: \texttt{Users}, \texttt{Scans}, \texttt{Recommendations}, and \texttt{SkillsCache}.

\subsubsection{1. Users Table (\texttt{users})}
Stores the active local user profile and preferences.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Column} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Constraints} & \textbf{Description} \\ \midrule
\texttt{id} & TEXT & PRIMARY KEY & UUID v4 identifier \\
\texttt{full\_name} & TEXT & NOT NULL & User full name \\
\texttt{email} & TEXT & UNIQUE, NOT NULL & Contact email address \\
\texttt{education\_level} & TEXT & NULLABLE & Current academic or professional standing \\
\texttt{created\_at} & DATETIME & DEFAULT CURRENT\_TIMESTAMP & Record creation timestamp \\
\texttt{updated\_at} & DATETIME & DEFAULT CURRENT\_TIMESTAMP & Last profile update timestamp \\ \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsubsection{2. Scans Table (\texttt{scans})}
Persists raw and normalized results from the camera scanner interface.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Column} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Constraints} & \textbf{Description} \\ \midrule
\texttt{id} & TEXT & PRIMARY KEY & UUID v4 identifier \\
\texttt{user\_id} & TEXT & FOREIGN KEY $\to$ \texttt{users(id)} & Owner reference \\
\texttt{raw\_text} & TEXT & NOT NULL & Extracted optical text / parsed tokens \\
\texttt{source\_type} & TEXT & CHECK(source\_type IN ('resume', 'transcript', 'manual')) & Input modality \\
\texttt{synced} & INTEGER & DEFAULT 0 & Sync flag (0 = local only, 1 = synced to API) \\
\texttt{scanned\_at} & DATETIME & DEFAULT CURRENT\_TIMESTAMP & Capture timestamp \\ \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsubsection{3. Recommendations Table (\texttt{recommendations})}
Caches match outcomes generated by the matching engine.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Column} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Constraints} & \textbf{Description} \\ \midrule
\texttt{id} & TEXT & PRIMARY KEY & UUID v4 identifier \\
\texttt{scan\_id} & TEXT & FOREIGN KEY $\to$ \texttt{scans(id)} & Originating scan reference \\
\texttt{career\_title} & TEXT & NOT NULL & Suggested role or target trajectory \\
\texttt{match\_score} & REAL & NOT NULL & Normalized confidence score (0.00 -- 1.00) \\
\texttt{gap\_summary} & TEXT & NOT NULL & JSON array of missing prerequisite skills \\
\texttt{created\_at} & DATETIME & DEFAULT CURRENT\_TIMESTAMP & Engine execution timestamp \\ \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsubsection{4. Skills Cache Table (\texttt{skills\_cache})}
Maintains standardized taxonomy tokens to power offline autocomplete and parsing heuristics.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Column} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Constraints} & \textbf{Description} \\ \midrule
\texttt{id} & INTEGER & PRIMARY KEY AUTOINCREMENT & Internal surrogate key \\
\texttt{skill\_name} & TEXT & UNIQUE, NOT NULL & Standardized skill label \\
\texttt{category} & TEXT & NOT NULL & Domain (e.g., 'Frontend', 'Database', 'Cloud') \\
\texttt{frequency} & INTEGER & DEFAULT 1 & Usage frequency index \\ \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Data Access \& Transaction Protocol}
All writes into \texttt{scans} and \texttt{recommendations} must execute within transactional blocks to guarantee ACID compliance during network disruptions. Foreign key constraints must be strictly enforced:
\begin{verbatim}
PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON;
\end{verbatim}
